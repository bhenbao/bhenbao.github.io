Projects

A few things I've built.

Mars Robot

Over a 6 week period, I worked in a team of 4 to build an autonomous mars robot from scratch for a robotics competition. I worked on the mechanical arm and claw, chassis, PCBs, and PID tuning, enabling our robot to precisely detect and pick up objects.

Antenna Deployer Module

As a part of UBC Orbit, a CubeSat design team, I lead the design structural design of our antenna deployer module. This encompasses custom designing a burn-wire door release mechanism, integrating electrical components, and conducting tests.

View project ↗

Cell Phone Stand

In UBC Physics & Astronomy's Student Machine Shop course, I learned to operate a drill press, lathe, and mill, from facing and threading to edge finding and milling. Combined with waterjet cutting and reading detailed drawing, I applied all these skills to machine a folding, angle-adjustable cell phone stand.
